Five quiz questions and a coach that decides when you should review

After every answer, Üki the owl updates how likely you are to know the topic, which learning stage you are in, and whether a review or a new question will help you more.

    How Üki decides

    1. Bayesian inference turns your answer into an updated probability that you know the topic.
    2. Hidden Markov model and Viterbi read your answers together and find the most likely path of learning stages, Beginner or Ready.
    3. Markov decision process compares the long-term value of Review and Next question in your current stage and recommends the better one.

    One model links them: the HMM moves between stages with the same probabilities the MDP uses for Next question, and a review changes those probabilities for the next answer.
